# Security

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in NEXORA, report it to
security@onenexora.com rather than disclosing it publicly. Give us a
reasonable opportunity to investigate and address a report before any public
disclosure, and don't access or modify data that isn't your own while
investigating.

The full policy — what we actually have in place today, and what we
haven't done yet — is published at https://onenexora.com/legal/security.
Machine-readable contact info is at
https://onenexora.com/.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116).
